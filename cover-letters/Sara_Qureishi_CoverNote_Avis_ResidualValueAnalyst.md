# Avis Budget Group — Graduate Residual Value Analyst
## Cover note / "Why this role, why Avis"

Dear Hiring Team,

I am applying for the Graduate Residual Value Analyst role because it sits right where I want to build my career: using data to make real commercial decisions. I am completing an MSc in Artificial Intelligence, and the part of my degree I enjoy most is not the modelling for its own sake, it is taking messy data, working out what it is actually telling you, and turning that into a recommendation someone can act on. Forecasting the future value of a large vehicle fleet is exactly that kind of problem, and it is one where the quality of the analysis has a direct effect on the numbers the business lives by.

What draws me to residual values specifically is that it combines data with a real market. Vehicle values move with mileage, condition, model cycles, fuel type and wider demand, and the shift towards electric vehicles is changing the picture again. I find that genuinely interesting, because it means the analysis is never just a spreadsheet exercise. You have to understand what is happening in the market and stay curious about why the numbers move. I would rather work on something that connects to the real world like this than on an abstract problem.

I think I would fit the role well. In my previous job as an analytics consultant I spent two years collecting and interpreting data from different sources, running quality and validation checks, and explaining what I found to colleagues who were not technical. I am comfortable in Excel, SQL and Power BI, and I am used to being the person who notices when a number looks wrong and digs into why before it goes any further. I am also honest about what the data can and cannot tell you, which I think matters a lot when your analysis feeds into pricing and forecasting.

I am at the start of my career and I know I have a lot to learn about the automotive and fleet side, but I am keen to learn it properly and I pick things up quickly. I would welcome the chance to do that at Avis Budget Group and to grow into the commercial side of the role over time.

Thank you for considering my application.

Kind regards,
Sara Qureishi
